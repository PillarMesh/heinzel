# PillarMesh Managed Data Engineering Platform Design Record

**Status:** Approved direction; canonical addendum proposed for final review

**Date:** 2026-08-17

The validated design from the managed-platform brainstorming is recorded canonically in:

`docs/architecture/specifications/managed-data-engineering-platform-addendum-v0.1.md`

That addendum captures the approved decisions and MVP scope for:

- Fivetran-category provider-managed integrations;
- mandatory PillarMesh-operated PostgreSQL or ClickHouse warehouse;
- one-person data engineering supervised autopilot;
- a data engineering architect journey from managed environment setup through daily inbox operations;
- unified stakeholder questions, access requests, changes, incidents, proposals, and risk-tiered approvals;
- business-process modeling before Integration Contract compilation;
- managed OpenMetadata with future catalog federation;
- managed transformations and warehouse integrity layers;
- managed Apache Superset dashboards and periodic reports;
- scheduling through narrow idempotent trigger policies;
- on-premises source relay and future customer-infrastructure placement;
- source-to-consumer evidence, recovery, and exit portability;
- business-process upload with attributable extraction, semantic review, and contract compilation;
- governed stakeholder answers and least-privilege access fulfillment;
- a bounded architect-operated MVP witnessed with a revenue-to-cash fixture, PostgreSQL and Stripe sources, customer-selectable managed PostgreSQL or ClickHouse, OpenMetadata, and Superset; and
- post-MVP evidence gates for connector, catalog, schedule, process, placement, and scale expansion.

Historical Snowflake M0 specifications remain evidence of the earlier thin-thread experiment. The new addendum supersedes their product destination, warehouse-ownership, catalog, BI, and scheduling assumptions for post-M0 implementation planning without rewriting their historical claims.
